Yahora

A minimalist event logger for Android

You give a recurring event a name, and from then on a single tap on its chip records the date and time it happened. No intermediate screen, no confirmation dialog, no extra fields to fill in. A tag has a name and a favourite flag; an entry has a timestamp. That is the entire model.

Yahora is fully offline: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network permission at all.
